Kässpätzla

Kässpätzla are a swabian dish that every german loves

Incridients

  • 1000g Spätzla
  • 200g Cheese
  • 300ml Creme Cheese
  • 4 small Onions
  • 4 tbsp Oil
  • 4 tbsp Chieves
  • Pepper
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Bring water to boil and add Salt to it.
  2. Put Spätzla into the boiling water.
  3. Cut the onions and fry it in a pan with Oil until golden brown, then remove it.
  4. Put the cooked Spätzla into the pan and add cheese. Don't forget to stir.
  5. Soon as the cheese is melted, turn off the heat and give the creme cheese to it.
  6. When it's creamy we're ready to serve. Before that we add the onions and cutted chieves on top of our Kässpätzla
