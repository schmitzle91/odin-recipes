Rote Wurst

Every swabian Barbecue or Sunday League match should include a rote Wurst with mustard in a bun

Incridients

  • 1 bun
  • 1 Rote Wurst
  • 1 tpsp mustard

Steps

  1. Heat up the grill and scratch the Rote Wurst every 2cm and about 3mm deep
  2. Cut the Bun in the half but not through
  3. Grill th Wurst until it gets brown
  4. Put the Wurst into the bun and add mustard or ketchup on it
